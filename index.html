Welcome

A family lodge, not a franchise

Since 1998, the Molengoane family has welcomed travellers, wedding parties, church retreats, and government delegations to this corner of Nazareth. Far enough from Maseru to breathe mountain air — close enough to arrive in under an hour.

Whether you're heading to Mohale Dam, riding the Roof of Africa route, or gathering your team for a strategy retreat, you'll find a clean bed, a warm meal, and people who remember your name.

Explore from the Lodge

The highlands begin at our door

Molengoane sits on the A3 Mountain Road — the gateway to some of Lesotho's most loved places. Sleep here, explore everywhere.

Ha Baroana Rock Paintings± 7 kmAncient San rock art in a river gorge — one of Lesotho's most important heritage sites.
Dinosaur Footprints± 10 kmWalk in 200-million-year-old tracks pressed into the sandstone.
Qiloane Falls & Pony Trekking± 15 kmWaterfalls and guided Basotho pony treks from Setibing village.
Thaba Bosiu Cultural Village± 19 kmThe mountain fortress of King Moshoeshoe I — the birthplace of the Basotho nation.
Mohale Dam± 40 kmBoat trips, fishing and dramatic highland scenery on the Mountain Road.
Semonkong Falls± 80 kmMaletsunyane Falls — a 192 m single drop, and the world's longest commercial abseil.
